## Structure Breakdown

### HOOK (0:00 - 0:05)

**Purpose**: Create immediate curiosity about the "mistake"

**Beat**: State the outcome (deadliest collapse) and tease the cause (30-second approval)

**Word count**: ~20 words

**Key elements**:
- Dramatic outcome stated upfront
- Specific detail (30 seconds) creates curiosity
- "No one checked the math" implies human error

---

### CONTEXT (0:05 - 1:30)

**Purpose**: Set the scene, introduce the location, establish the stakes

**Beats**:

1. **The Setting** (0:05 - 0:30)
   - Describe the Hyatt Regency
   - Emphasize the impressive atrium
   - Visual: sweeping architecture
   - Word count: ~60

2. **The Walkways** (0:30 - 0:50)
   - Describe the suspended walkway design
   - Note they were a visual feature
   - Visual: walkways from below
   - Word count: ~50

3. **The Night** (0:50 - 1:15)
   - Tea dance event
   - 1,600 people present
   - Crowd on walkways watching
   - Visual: archival photos if available
   - Word count: ~60

4. **The Collapse** (1:15 - 1:30)
   - Brief, factual description
   - 114 dead, 216 injured
   - Establish this is the story we're explaining
   - Word count: ~50

**Total context word count**: ~220

---

### ESCALATION (1:30 - 8:00)

**Purpose**: Build understanding through progressive reveals. Each beat adds a layer of insight.

---

#### Beat 1: The Original Design (1:30 - 3:00)

**What happens**: Explain how the walkways were supposed to be built

**Visual**: Diagram of original design (single continuous rod)

**Key points**:
- Single rod through both walkways
- Load transfers directly to ceiling
- Math was correct
- But construction was difficult (40-foot rod)

**Stakes escalation**: Viewer understands the baseline design worked

**Word count**: ~200

---

#### Beat 2: The Phone Call (3:00 - 4:30)

**What happens**: The contractor suggests a change, engineer approves

**Visual**: Split-rod diagram, phone conversation illustration

**Key points**:
- Contractor suggests two shorter rods
- Easier to build
- Approved in ~30 seconds
- No recalculation done

**Stakes escalation**: Viewer realizes something went wrong here

**Emotional note**: Building tension—we know the outcome

**Word count**: ~200

---

#### Beat 3: The Hidden Flaw (4:30 - 6:00)

**What happens**: Explain why the change was catastrophic

**Visual**: Load diagram showing doubled stress on connection

**Key points**:
- New design doubled load on fourth-floor connection
- Original: connection holds one walkway
- Modified: connection holds both walkways
- The math was never checked

**Stakes escalation**: Viewer now understands the failure mechanism

**Technical clarity**: Use simple analogy if needed (chain vs. two hooks)

**Word count**: ~200

---

#### Beat 4: The Year Before (6:00 - 7:00)

**What happens**: The walkways were used normally for over a year

**Visual**: Normal hotel operations, people walking

**Key points**:
- No visible warnings
- Daily use
- The flaw was invisible
- Waiting for the right (wrong) conditions

**Stakes escalation**: Dread—the trap is set

**Word count**: ~150

---

#### Beat 5: The Breaking Point (7:00 - 8:00)

**What happens**: The night of the collapse, moment of failure

**Visual**: Crowded walkway, stress diagram, then collapse simulation

**Key points**:
- Crowded conditions added weight
- Connection exceeded capacity
- Steel deformed, then tore
- Cascade failure in under 3 seconds

**Stakes escalation**: Peak tension, release

**Word count**: ~150

---

### PAYOFF (8:00 - 9:30)

**Purpose**: Deliver the investigation findings and accountability

**Beats**:

1. **The Investigation** (8:00 - 8:45)
   - NBS engineers recreated failure
   - Modified design at 53% of code requirement
   - Phone approval violated practice
   - Word count: ~120

2. **The Accountability** (8:45 - 9:15)
   - Two engineers lost licenses
   - First time in Missouri history
   - Firm sued out of existence
   - Word count: ~100

3. **The Changes** (9:15 - 9:30)
   - Building codes updated
   - Verification procedures strengthened
   - Word count: ~50

**Total payoff word count**: ~270

---

### WRAP (9:30 - 10:00)

**Purpose**: Extract the universal lesson

**Beat**:

- This wasn't unusual—routine shortcut in complex system
- Systems fail when verification is skipped
- 30 seconds vs. 1 hour
- The math matters

**Tone**: Reflective, not preachy

**Word count**: ~80

**No**: Calls to action, like/subscribe requests, channel mentions
