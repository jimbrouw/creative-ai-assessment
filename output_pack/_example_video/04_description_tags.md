## Description

### Above the Fold (First 2-3 lines - most important)

In 1981, the Hyatt Regency Kansas City suffered the deadliest structural collapse in American history. 114 people died because of a phone call that lasted 30 seconds. This is the story of how a minor construction change—approved without verification—doubled the load on a critical connection and caused catastrophic failure.

### Main Body

The Hyatt Regency walkway collapse remains a defining case study in engineering ethics and professional responsibility. The disaster led to the first-ever revocation of engineering licenses for structural failure and changed building codes nationwide.

In this video, we examine:
• The original walkway design and why it worked
• The phone call that changed everything
• How a "minor" modification doubled the structural load
• Why the flaw remained hidden for over a year
• The investigation that exposed the failure
• The lasting impact on engineering practice
