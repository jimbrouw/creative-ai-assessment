## Stock Sources

### Primary (Best for this topic)
- **Storyblocks**: General construction, hotel interiors
- **Pond5**: Period-appropriate (1980s), archival news
- **Artgrid**: High-quality modern B-roll

### Archival Sources
- **Archive.org**: Public domain historical footage
- **Getty Images**: Premium archival (if budget allows)
- **AP Archive**: News footage

### Notes
- Avoid footage with visible modern elements (smartphones, modern cars) for 1981 scenes
- Hotel footage should look "1980s-appropriate" where possible
- Engineering/construction footage should emphasize steel and connections
